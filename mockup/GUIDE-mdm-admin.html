What an admin cannot do

mdm-admin holds every privilege: read, edit, approve, publish, configure. Separation of duties still applies, and admins are not exempt.

You cannot approve a change request you contributed to. Approve shows “you contributed to this batch”. Editing any record makes you a contributor to your draft.

You cannot give the second approval to an in-year manifest you built. Second approval shows “you built this manifest”.
